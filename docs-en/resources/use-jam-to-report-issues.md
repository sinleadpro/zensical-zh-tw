## Before You Start

 - Include reproduction steps and related product or action details so we can resolve the issue faster.
 - Before you record or take screenshots, remove or mask all sensitive data so no personal information appears.

!!! tip "A picture or video beats a thousand words"
    Just capture or record **what you see, what you do, and what happens** to make issue reports simpler, clearer, and more effective.

## Steps

### Install the Jam Chrome Extension

1. Open the [Jam page in the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/jam/iohjgamcilhbgmhbnllfolmkmmekfmci).
2. Click **Add to Chrome > Add extension** to finish installing.
3. Click the puzzle icon in the top-right of Chrome and pin Jam to the toolbar for quick access. (Recommended)
4. Click the Jam 🍓 icon, then sign in with Google or create a Jam account.

![](../assets/images/install-jam-extension.en.gif)

---

### Record and Reproduce the Issue

1. Click the Jam 🍓 icon in the top-right of the Chrome toolbar.  
2. Choose a screenshot or a recording:

	- **:material-camera-plus-outline: Capture Screenshot**: Capture the current page state and keep the full technical context.
	- **:material-tab: Record Tab**: Record on-screen actions to walk through the issue step by step.
	> To record audio, click the microphone icon :material-microphone-off:/:material-microphone: and turn the mic on.
	- **:material-skip-backward: Instant Replay**: Automatically capture the last 30 seconds of activity so you do not need to reproduce the error again.   

3. Reproduce the issue. You can also narrate what you are doing.  
4. When you finish recording, click End Recording. Jam generates a video link you can share with support or engineering.

![](../assets/images/jam-capture-options.en.png)

---

### Share the Video with Support and Engineering

1. Click **Share** or **Copy link**.  
2. Email the link to CYBERBIZ support: [support@cyberbiz.io](mailto:support@cyberbiz.io).
3. If you need to include device specs, open system info in Jam settings and share it as well.  

## FAQ

??? quote "Is Jam free?"
    Yes. Basic recording and sharing are free.

??? quote "Will the video be public?"
    Only people with the link can watch it, and you can delete it.

??? quote "I recorded the error but there is no audio. What should I do?"
    Make sure microphone permission was granted while recording.
