This table lists the shipping label types that CYBERBIZ integrated logistics can generate, for reference in shipping, search, and filters. Custom logistics (your own delivery or partner carriers) aren't listed. For those, mark the order as shipped manually and enter the tracking number.

## Integrated Logistics Categories { #shipping-methods }

| Category | Delivery Type | Description |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Home delivery | EZCAT TA-Q-BIN | Ambient home delivery to the recipient address |
| Home delivery | Pelican Express | Ambient home delivery to the recipient address |
| Home delivery | HCT Logistics | Ambient home delivery to the recipient address |
| Home delivery | SF Express | Home delivery, with overseas delivery support |
| Convenience store pickup | 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Hi-Life | The customer picks up at a convenience store |
| Convenience store store-to-store | 7-Eleven Store-to-Store, FamilyMart Store-to-Store, Hi-Life Store-to-Store | Store-to-store drop-off; the customer picks up at the store |
| Convenience store frozen / cold chain | FamilyMart Cold Chain, FamilyMart Frozen Store-to-Store, Hi-Life Frozen | Convenience store pickup for chilled and frozen products |
| Quick Store Delivery | EZCAT Quick Store Delivery (Ambient / Refrigerated / Frozen) | EZCAT delivers to a convenience store for pickup |
| Store pickup | Store pickup barcode slip | The customer picks up at the merchant's store |
| Cross-border / overseas | DHL, LINEX, SF Express (overseas) | International / cross-border delivery |
<!-- | On-demand delivery | Pandago, Uber Direct | Same-city on-demand delivery | -->

!!! note "Notes"
    * The delivery types you can choose depend on the logistics and plan you've turned on. Not every store has all of the options above.
    * Thermal printing of convenience store shipping labels requires a PLUS or Enterprise plan.
    * Custom logistics aren't in this table. For logistics that aren't integrated, use custom logistics shipping to mark orders as shipped manually and enter the tracking number.
