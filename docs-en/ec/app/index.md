# CYBERBIZ APP

Extend your online store to mobile devices and build a member engagement channel you can reach again and again with push notifications, a store map, and a first-login gift.
{ .subtitle }

[:lucide-grid-2x2-plus:{ title="Applicable Add-ons" }](../../resources/conventions.md#適用擴充) | CYBERBIZ APP
{ .doc-badge }

!!! tip "Use Cases"
    - **A Fixed Brand Entry Point**: Use a mobile app as an extension of your online store to make it easier for members to come back.
    - **Reach Members Proactively**: Send push notifications about orders, campaigns, and member benefits.
    - **Drive Downloads and First Logins**: Offer a first-login gift to increase app downloads and first-login conversions.

The online store app inherits the visuals and product data of your web store and integrates the membership card, coupon, and bonus points features. Work through the sections below in order: learn about the app, build it, and then engage your members.
