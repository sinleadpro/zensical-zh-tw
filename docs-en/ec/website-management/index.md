## Invoices and Reconciliation { #overview-wm-hub-billing }

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :lucide-receipt: __Wixtar E-Invoice Setup__

    ---

    Buy Wixtar e-invoices and connect the service. Share it across stores, and set up reconciliation invoices in the CYBERBIZ admin panel.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go](wixtar-e-invoice-setup.md)

-   :lucide-file-text: __Manage System-Issued Consumer Invoices__

    ---

    CYBERBIZ can issue consumer invoices for you. Learn how they are issued and how customers and merchants find them. See the deadlines and fees to change invoice details.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go](manage-system-issued-consumer-invoices.md)

-   :lucide-receipt: __One-Click Invoice Issuance__

    ---

    Turn on and use one-click billing invoice issuance. Enterprise merchants with a Wixtar account can issue invoices with one click in the Reconciliation Center. This speeds up billing and payouts.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go](one-click-invoice-issuance.md)

-   :lucide-circle-check: __Set Up Automatic Confirmation of Reconciliation Statements__

    ---

    With CYBERBIZ PAYMENTS, you must confirm each statement by hand before you get paid. Turn on automatic statement confirmation and the system does it for you on the closing date, once the total reaches your threshold. Your payouts keep coming on time.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go](auto-confirm-reconciliation-accounts.md)

-   :lucide-credit-card: __Set Up Automatic Deduction of Arrears__

    ---

    If you use CYBERBIZ PAYMENTS and your statement balance is negative, the system charges your linked credit card through automatic arrears deduction. This keeps your store working normally.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go](auto-deduction-of-arrears.md)

## Troubleshooting { #overview-wm-hub-troubleshoot }

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :lucide-mail-warning: __Unable to Receive the Admin Panel Login Verification Email__

    ---

    Not getting the verification email when you log in to the admin panel? Follow this guide to fix it fast.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go](not-receiving-backend-login-verification-email.md)

-   :lucide-mail-x: __Troubleshoot Missing Merchant Notification Emails__

    ---

    Are emails from CYBERBIZ, such as order notices and system news, going to Spam? This guide helps you change your settings so you get them on time.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go](troubleshoot-not-receiving-merchant-notifications.md)

-   :lucide-shield-alert: __Cloudflare SSL Conflict__

    ---

    Cloudflare is a well-known DNS host and CDN. If your domain is on Cloudflare, set its Proxy feature a certain way when you point the domain to CYBERBIZ. Then your website loads and the SSL certificate works.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go](cloudflare-ssl-conflict.md)

-   :lucide-globe: __Unable to Load the HTTPS Root URL__

    ---

    Moved your website to CYBERBIZ? http://your-domain may open while https://your-domain does not. This often depends on whether your domain registrar supports redirects for the root domain (naked domain).

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go](unable-to-read-https-root-url.md)

</div>
