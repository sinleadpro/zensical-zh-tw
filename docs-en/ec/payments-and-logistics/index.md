## Cross-Border E-Commerce { #overview-payments-hub-xb }

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :lucide-globe: __North America Payment Services__

    ---

    The North America site integrates mainstream international payment solutions, including credit card and e-wallet payments, as well as the widely used PayPal service, helping merchants operate in the US market smoothly.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](north-america-payment-services.md)

-   :lucide-globe: __Japan Payment Services__

    ---

    The Japan site provides localized payment solutions, including mainstream credit card payments and Pay-Easy, a service unique to Japan, helping merchants enter the Japanese e-commerce market smoothly.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](japan-payment-services.md)

-   :lucide-globe: __Southeast Asia Payment Services__

    ---

    CYBERBIZ provides standard credit card service and also integrates the leading local payment provider Fiuu (formerly Razer Merchant Services), supporting bank transfers, e-wallets, and convenience store cash payments.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](southeast-asia-payment-services.md)

-   :lucide-globe: __Australia Local Payment Services__

    ---

    The Australia local version provides merchants with stable and diverse local payment services through its partnership with Airwallex. This guide walks you through the full process, from account application and website compliance review to API integration in the admin.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](australia-local-payment-services.md)

-   :lucide-package: __DHL Cross-Border Logistics__

    ---

    Integrate DHL Express through the CYBERBIZ admin to generate English-language shipping labels and commercial invoices directly, with home pickup and automated shipment tracking.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](dhl-cross-border-logistics.md)

-   :lucide-package: __LINEX Cross-Border Logistics__

    ---

    LINEX offers flexible cross-border logistics. Merchants can choose between "home pickup" and "self-delivery to warehouse" modes, and the admin automatically generates the shipping labels and related documents required for customs clearance.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](linex-cross-border-logistics.md)

-   :lucide-plane: __SF Express International Logistics__

    ---

    Integrate SF Express International Logistics through the CYBERBIZ admin to generate cross-border shipping labels directly. The format automatically switches between Chinese and English based on the recipient address.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](sf-express-overseas-logistics.md)

-   :lucide-building: __Amazon FBA Cross-Border Logistics__

    ---

    With Amazon FBA, you can have Amazon's logistics team pack and ship your website orders. The system automatically sends orders, syncs delivery status, and updates inventory to automate cross-border operations.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](amazon-fba-cross-border-logistics.md)

</div>
