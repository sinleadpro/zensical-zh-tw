## Payment and Logistics Settings { #overview-payments-settings }

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :lucide-shopping-cart: __Cart Settings__

    ---

    Adjust customer cart behavior before checkout, including cart activation, abandoned cart reminders, coupon settings, additional information fields, and recurring-payment cart settings.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](cart-settings.md)

-   :lucide-receipt: __Order Settings__

    ---

    Set the minimum order amount, unpaid and failed-payment reminders, automatic closing and auto-cancellation rules, customer self-service cancellation and return permissions, and print content for order details and picking lists.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](order-settings.md)

-   :lucide-truck: __Logistics Settings__

    ---

    Adjust delivery details and the delivery preferences customers can select, including convenience store pickup name and offshore island settings, shipping detail printing, delivery date and time slot selection, return period days, and Uber Direct.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Go to](logistics-settings.md)

</div>
