!!! note "Notes"
    * Actual receiving time slots at each logistics center follow official announcements and may change during public holidays.
    * For FamilyMart frozen store pickup, confirm receiving details with customer service after your application is approved.
    * After goods arrive at the store, the system sends a pickup SMS to the consumer automatically on Day 1 and Day 4.

## Warehouse Receiving Inspection Rules { #reference-cvs-b2c-acceptance-rules }

Receiving inspection rules for each channel are as follows:

=== "7-ELEVEN Store Pickup (Dazhitong)"

    1. **Outer Box Protection**: Goods must not be loose and must be protected by an outer carton. If goods are damaged or lost due to lack of carton protection, the merchant bears full responsibility.
    2. **Carton Count Inspection**: After the driver and receiving staff complete the handover, both parties sign the Goods Receipt Log and the Freight Delivery Receipt for record.
    3. **Goods Inspection**: If the outer bag is damaged or the packaging is poor, the goods will be treated as returned merchandise and sent back to the store.
    4. **Delivery Rules**: Private cars, sedans, and pedestrians are not allowed to enter the facility to deliver goods. Goods must be transported by a **3.5-ton or larger truck**. Logistics centers will refuse all non-compliant deliveries.
    5. **Weekly Return-to-Vendor Rules**: When collecting return packages, follow each carrier's pickup schedule.

        - Monday: No return pickups accepted
        - Tuesday: Hsinchu Logistics, Kerry TJ Logistics (the logistics center returns return-to-vendor packages on its own)
        - Wednesday: Other carriers (merchants must notify the cooperating carrier to collect return-to-vendor packages from the logistics center)
        - Thursday: Tcat (the logistics center returns return-to-vendor packages on its own)
        - Friday: Pelican Express (the logistics center returns return-to-vendor packages on its own)

    !!! info "For detailed rules, refer to Dazhitong's announcements."

=== "FamilyMart Store Pickup (Riyi)"

    1. **Outer Box Protection**: Goods must not be loose and must be protected by an outer carton. If goods are lost or damaged due to lack of carton protection, the merchant bears full responsibility.
    2. **Carton Count Inspection**: After the driver and receiving staff complete the handover, both parties sign the Goods Receipt Log for record.
    3. **Piece Count Inspection**: Based on the actual scan count by Riyi Logistics. If the outer bag is damaged or the packaging is poor, the goods will be treated as returned merchandise and sent back to the store.
