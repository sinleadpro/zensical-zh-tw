# Member Age Groups Reference

"Member Age Groups" on the "Member Overview" tab groups members into the following ranges by the age calculated from their birthday.

### Age Ranges { #reference-member-age-groups }

| Age Range | Description |
| :-- | :-- |
| 19 and under | Calculated age is under 20 |
| 20-29 | Ages 20 to 29 |
| 30-39 | Ages 30 to 39 |
| 40-49 | Ages 40 to 49 |
| 50-59 | Ages 50 to 59 |
| 60-69 | Ages 60 to 69 |
| 70 and over | Age 70 or older |
| Not provided | The member has not entered a birthday, so age cannot be calculated |

!!! note "Note"
    * Age is **calculated in real time** from the birthday the member entered, not the age at registration.
    * Members without a birthday are grouped under "Not provided." If this group is large, many members have not filled in their birthday yet. Encourage them to add it as part of your member engagement to make segmentation more accurate.
