# Member Level Sales Status Reference

"Member Level Sales Status by Period" on the "Member Overview" tab splits members into two groups by level and compares the sales contribution of each.

### Member Levels { #reference-member-levels }

| Level | What It Means for Merchants |
| :-- | :-- |
| VIP Members | Members who have met the VIP conditions you set and been upgraded to VIP |
| Regular Members | Members who have not yet met the VIP conditions |


!!! note "Note"
    * The line between VIP and regular members depends on the thresholds in your member level settings (for example, cumulative spending or number of purchases). This report only summarizes the sales of the two groups based on those settings.
    * Comparing the sales contribution of VIP and regular members helps you judge whether your current VIP threshold and membership program are working. If VIP members contribute much more, your level system is effectively concentrating on your core customers.
