## What Is Google Business Profile? { #intro-google-business }

Creating a [My Business Profile :lucide-external-link:](https://business.google.com/tw/business-profile/) (Google Business Profile) is an important step for physical stores and brand owners. It is a free tool that lets merchants manage their information on Google Search and Google Maps, increasing visibility and building customer trust.

*   **Core features**: Lets merchants display official information on Google Search and Maps, helping potential customers find you faster.
*   **Knowledge panel**: After you create and verify your profile, a Knowledge Panel may appear on the right side of search results when users search for your brand, showing your brand's official details.
*   **Important limitation**: **Only merchants with a physical store or who offer in-person services** can register this profile. Learn about [Merchant eligibility :lucide-external-link:](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?sjid=3926033107048309425-NAA).

## Step-by-Step Setup Guide { #operate-google-business }

!!! warning "This feature is provided and reviewed by Google. For the latest procedures and interface, refer to [Google's official information :lucide-external-link:](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2911778?hl=zh-Hant&ref_topic=4596754&sjid=3926033107048309425-NA)."

1.  **Turn on the feature**: Sign in to [Google Maps :lucide-external-link:](https://www.google.com/maps), click the **☰** menu on the left sidebar, and select **Add your business**.
2.  **Basic information**: Enter your **Business name** and **Business category**.
3.  **Location settings**: Choose whether to add a physical location that customers can visit in person.
4.  **Service area**: If you offer delivery or in-home services, enter your service area.
5.  **Contact information**: Enter your phone number, website URL, and other contact details.
6.  **Updates**: Choose whether to receive business-related updates and recommendations.
7.  **Verify your identity**: Enter your **Mailing address** to start the verification process.
8.  **Business information**: Set your **Business hours** and write a short **Business description**.
9.  **Business photos**: Add photos of your store or product images to make your profile more appealing.
10. **Finish setup**: Click **Continue**, then wait for Google to **verify** your profile. Once verified, your business profile goes live.

## FAQ { #faq-google-business }

??? quote "Who can create a Google Business Profile?"

    Only merchants with a **physical store** or who offer **in-person services** can create a Google Business Profile. For details, see the [official eligibility guidelines :lucide-external-link:](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=zh-Hant&ref_topic=4540086&sjid=3926033107048309425-NA).

??? quote "How long does verification take?"

    Google mails a verification code by postcard to the address you provide. The process usually takes 5-14 business days. Enter the code after you receive it to complete verification.

??? quote "What information can I add after creating a profile?"

    You can add your business name, business category, business hours, phone number, website URL, business description, and product or store photos.
