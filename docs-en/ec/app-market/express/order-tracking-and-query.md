# CYBERBIZ EXPRESS Shipment Tracking and Lookup
Learn how merchants and customers track shipping status with CYBERBIZ EXPRESS, and how to view estimated and actual shipping fees for each consignment note.
{ .subtitle }

[:lucide-layers:{ title="Applicable Products" }](../../resources/conventions#適用產品) | Cross-Border E-Commerce (Japan to Taiwan)<br>
[:lucide-grid-2x2-plus:{ title="Applicable Add-ons" }](../../resources/conventions#適用擴充) | CYBERBIZ EXPRESS
{ .doc-badge }
## Shipment Tracking { #operate-order-tracking }

=== "Merchants"

    1. Go to **前往APP MARKET > My Apps > CYBERBIZ EXPRESS**.
    2. Open the **Tracking Number** tab and click a tracking number link.
    3. Look it up on the [CYBERBIZ EXPRESS tracking page](https://www.cyberbiz.express/zh-TW).
        - You can look up as many as 10 consignment notes at a time.
        - The page is available in Chinese, English, and Japanese.

        ![](../../../assets/images/EC-後台-APPMARKET-我的擴充服務-CYBERBIZEXPRESS-查看託運單配送狀態02.en.png){ .screenshot }


=== "Customers"

    1. Log in to the **Member Center** and click the **consignment note number** on the **Order Details** page.

        ![](../../../assets/images/EC-前台-訂單查詢-訂單明細-查看託運單號01.en.png){ .screenshot }

    2. On the [CYBERBIZ EXPRESS tracking page](https://www.cyberbiz.express/zh-TW), enter the consignment note number.
        - You can look up as many as 10 consignment notes at a time.
        - The page is available in Chinese, English, and Japanese.

        ![](../../../assets/images/EC-後台-APPMARKET-我的擴充服務-CYBERBIZEXPRESS-查看託運單配送狀態02.en.png){ .screenshot }


## View Consignment Note Shipping Fees { #operate-waybill-fee }

1. Log in to the admin and go to **APP MARKET > My Apps > CYBERBIZ EXPRESS**.


2. On the "Tracking Number" tab, view the estimated and actual shipping fee for each consignment note.

    ![](../../../assets/images/EC-後台-APPMARKET-我的擴充服務-CYBERBIZEXPRESS-查看託運單運費01.en.png){ .screenshot }
